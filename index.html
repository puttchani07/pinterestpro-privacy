Privacy Policy — Pinterest Pro

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Pinterest Pro is a browser extension designed to improve the Pinterest browsing experience through content filtering, board search, keyword blocking, appearance customization, and user-initiated image downloads.

1. Information We Collect

Pinterest Pro uses Supabase to provide account authentication and subscription management. We process account identifiers, including email addresses, to authenticate users and associate subscriptions with their accounts.

Paid subscriptions are processed through Stripe. We process subscription-related information, such as customer identifiers, subscription status, and billing period information, to manage access to paid features. Payment card details are entered into Stripe's payment interface and are not stored by Pinterest Pro.

2. Pinterest Content and Extension Settings

Pinterest Pro accesses Pinterest pages to provide its filtering, search, customization, and image download features.

Extension preferences, including filtering and display settings, may be stored locally in the browser. Pinterest images are downloaded only when users initiate a download action.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • Manage paid subscriptions and feature access.
  • Provide Pinterest filtering, search, customization, and download features.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the service.

We do not sell user information or use it for advertising, creditworthiness assessments, or lending decisions.

4. Third-Party Services

Pinterest Pro uses Supabase for authentication and subscription records and Stripe for payment processing and subscription management. These providers process information according to their applicable privacy policies.

Pinterest Pro is an independent extension and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Pinterest.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Account and subscription information may be retained while needed to operate the service, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, or maintain transaction records.

Users may request access to or deletion of their personal information by contacting the developer. Certain billing records may need to be retained for legal or accounting purposes.

6. Data Security

We use access controls and third-party service infrastructure to help protect account and subscription information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices or services change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
